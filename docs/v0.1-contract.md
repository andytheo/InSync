# InSync v0.1
Home → create/join → lobby → content → ready → watch.

Real-time: participant join/ready, content selected, play/pause/seek via PlaybackChanged, reactions, room end. Playback is server-authoritative and ignores stale sequence numbers.

Definition of done: two clients share a room; play on A reflects on B; pause on B reflects on A; seek converges; reactions cross the room.

Out of scope: database, auth, video calling, DRM bypass, rebroadcasting streaming content.
